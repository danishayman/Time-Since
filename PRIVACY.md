# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Time Since does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in, and there is no server.
- **No tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
- **On your device only.** Your timers, categories and settings are saved in the app's own storage
  on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Backups are yours.** If you export a backup, the app hands a JSON file to the Android share
  sheet and you choose where it goes. Importing reads only the file you pick.
- **Android backup.** If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the
  app's data in it, under Google's terms. Time Since plays no part in that.
- **Network.** The app only goes online when you tap "Source on GitHub" in Settings → About, which
  opens this repository in your browser.
